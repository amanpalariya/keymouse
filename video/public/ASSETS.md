Current photograph: `/Users/aman/Projects/key-mouse/video/public/tahoe-reference.jpg`

Reference screenshot: `/Users/aman/Projects/key-mouse/video/public/wikipedia-tahoe-dark.png`

The article [Lake Tahoe — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Tahoe) was inspected on 3 November 2026 in native Dark appearance. The screenshot is retained as a reference. The current video uses a simplified, code-native reading view with the real article's identity, verified facts and photograph.
